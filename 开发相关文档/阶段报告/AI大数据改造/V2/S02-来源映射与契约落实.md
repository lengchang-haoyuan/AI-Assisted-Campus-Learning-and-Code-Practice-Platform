## 2 可信教学关系条件

下面是已对本轮ACCEPT关系核对过的条件表达，供S03按既有仓储和只读一致性事务落实。它不是S02已实现的导出器。

```sql
SELECT f.id AS feedback_id, lr.id AS learning_record_id,
       s.id AS submission_id, v.version_number, sc.user_id
FROM feedback f
JOIN submissions s ON s.id = f.submission_id AND s.class_id = f.class_id
JOIN submission_versions v
  ON v.submission_id = f.submission_id AND v.version_number = f.version_number
JOIN class_memberships sm
  ON sm.id = s.student_membership_id AND sm.class_id = s.class_id
JOIN campus_memberships sc ON sc.id = sm.campus_membership_id
JOIN class_memberships tm
  ON tm.id = f.teacher_membership_id AND tm.class_id = f.class_id
  AND tm.member_role = 'teacher'
JOIN learning_records lr ON lr.id = f.learning_record_id AND lr.user_id = sc.user_id
WHERE f.decision = 'accept' AND v.status = 'accepted'
  AND sc.user_id = :owner_user_id;
```

教学来源记录必须有上述真实反馈、版本、教师和学生关系；metadata.source只是声明。教学确认指标则按同一快照中群组的最新版本与ACCEPT反馈统计，不要求学习记录仍存在。LearningRecord删除后的FK为SET NULL（结构已核，本轮按保留策略未删除成功记录），教师确认仍是独立事实。

普通API伪造source保留键、零分钟、NULL分钟或无时区occurred_at均实测422，未留下记录。历史零／脏来源／异常时间只供S03独立离线样例，不向库绕过校验灌入。
